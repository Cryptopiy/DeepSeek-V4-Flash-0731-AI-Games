TORNADO

Interactive Town Destruction Simulator

Guide a tornado through a small low-poly town. Watch roofs rip off, trees snap, and cars tumble into the vortex. Built with Three.js + cannon-es. Enjoy.

WASD / Arrows — move tornado over the ground
Q / E — lower / raise strength (1 → 5)
Shift — sprint (faster movement)
Space — pause / resume
C — cycle cameras (follow / orbit / overview)
R — reset the entire town
M — toggle sound  ·  Esc — menu / pause
